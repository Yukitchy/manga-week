Tokyo · Kyoto · Osaka · seven days

A week of manga, three cities.

Seven days built around the places where manga was made and the streets where it lives now. Guided start to finish, luggage forwarded hotel to hotel.

Day 1 · Tokyo · The district, floor by floor

Akihabara, top to bottom

One day in the anime and manga district, done properly: the big buildings everyone knows and the small shops most visitors never find. No queues for tickets, just walking and browsing.

The day

  1. 10:00
    Meet at Akihabara StationElectric Town exit. A quick map of the district and what each street is for.
  2. 10:15
    Radio KaikanTen floors of figures, trading cards and model kits. The best place to see what is popular this month.
  3. 11:30
    Mandarake ComplexEight floors of used manga, animation cels, vintage toys. Prices are honest and the finds are real.
  4. 12:30
    LunchSee the picks below.
  5. 14:00
    Retro games and the back streetsSuper Potato and the second-floor shops, where the district still looks like 2005.
  6. 15:30
    Maid cafeThirty minutes, one drink. It is part of the culture, and it is fun.
  7. 16:30
    Kanda Myojin shrineThe shrine that blesses electronics, ten minutes uphill. Anime-themed ema plaques and a quiet end to the day.
  8. 17:30
    DoneEvening free. Akihabara is loud until 20:00 if anyone wants more.

The route

Everything is on foot. About 3 km over the day, with plenty of sitting. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Where we eat

Kyushu Jangara Ramen AkihabaraAkihabara · tonkotsu ramenRich pork broth on the main street since 1984. Fast, loud, and exactly what the district eats.Open in Google Maps ↗Kanda MatsuyaKanda · soba since 1884Handmade buckwheat noodles in an old wooden shop, ten minutes from the station. Quiet and very Tokyo.Open in Google Maps ↗

Good for Fans of any depth. Shoppers. Anyone who wants to see the district with someone who knows it.

Keep in mind Sundays the main street is closed to cars and busier. Many shops open at 11:00, so mornings start with the big buildings.

Day 2 · Tokyo · Where manga was born

Tokiwa-so and Ikebukuro

The morning is the apartment where Tezuka, Fujiko Fujio and the other masters lived and drew in the 1950s, rebuilt exactly. The afternoon is Ikebukuro, the district that today serves fans as well as Akihabara does.

The day

  1. 10:00
    Tokiwa-so Manga MuseumShiinamachi, 20 minutes from Ikebukuro. The rebuilt 1952 apartment: the four-and-a-half-mat rooms, the shared kitchen, the stairs. Free entry, reserved slot.
  2. 11:30
    Tokiwa-so street walkThe neighbourhood monuments, the manga road, and the Chinese restaurant from the stories, still open.
  3. 12:30
    LunchSee the picks below.
  4. 14:00
    Animate Ikebukuro flagshipNine floors, the biggest anime shop in the world. Merchandise, books, the events floor.
  5. 15:30
    Otome RoadThe street of second-hand shops (K-Books, Lashinbang) where rare goods surface first.
  6. 17:00
    Pokemon Center Mega TokyoIn Sunshine City, five minutes away. Good for families and for gifts.
  7. 18:00
    DoneEvening free in Ikebukuro.

The route

Train Ikebukuro → Shiinamachi 5 min, then a 15-minute walk to the museum. Back to Ikebukuro by train. Afternoon on foot. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Where we eat

MatsubaShiinamachi · Chinese and ramenThe restaurant the Tokiwa-so artists ordered from, and drew into their manga. The same family runs it.Open in Google Maps ↗MutekiyaIkebukuro · tonkotsu ramenThe line outside is the review. Thick pork broth, big portions, twenty minutes from ordering to done.Open in Google Maps ↗

Good for Guests who want the history, not only the shopping. Good pacing after a full Akihabara day.

Keep in mind Tokiwa-so museum is closed on Mondays. Entry is free but reserved in advance.

Tokiwa-so Manga Museum ↗ Animate ↗

Day 3 · Tokyo · Ghibli, then the collectors’ arcade

Ghibli Museum and Nakano Broadway

The Ghibli Museum is the one place on the trip that needs planning months ahead, and it is worth it. The afternoon is Nakano Broadway, the arcade where Mandarake started, for original cels and rare comics.

The day

  1. 10:00
    Ghibli MuseumMitaka. Timed entry, tickets bought on the 10th of the previous month. The short film in the Saturn Theater, the Catbus room, the robot on the roof.
  2. 12:30
    Straw Hat Cafe and Inokashira ParkLunch at the museum cafe, then a walk through the park to Kichijoji station.
  3. 14:00
    Nakano BroadwayFour floors and thirty specialist shops: animation cels, vintage manga, watches, toys. Mandarake’s original home.
  4. 16:30
    Daily ChikoThe eight-flavour soft serve in the basement. A Nakano rite of passage.
  5. 17:00
    DoneNakano is five minutes from Shinjuku for the evening.

The route

Train to Mitaka 30 min from Shinjuku, then a 15-minute walk or the museum bus. Kichijoji → Nakano 10 min by train. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Where we eat

Straw Hat CafeGhibli Museum · cafeInside the museum. Simple, good food: the omelette, the cutlet sandwich, the museum beer. Terrace tables outside.Open in Google Maps ↗Daily ChikoNakano Broadway · soft serveThe tallest soft-serve cone in Tokyo, eight flavours stacked. Tiny stand in the basement food hall.Open in Google Maps ↗

Good for Ghibli fans, families, anyone who loves the films. Collectors get their best afternoon here.

Keep in mind Ghibli Museum is closed Tuesdays. Tickets sell out in minutes, so the trip dates are set around this day. No photos inside the museum.

Ghibli Museum ↗ Nakano Broadway ↗

Day 4 · Tokyo → Kyoto · Fujiko F. Fujio Museum to Kyoto

Doraemon, then the bullet train

A morning with Doraemon at the museum built for him in Kawasaki, then the Shinkansen to Kyoto with the luggage already on its way hotel-to-hotel. Evening in the old shopping arcades and Gion.

The day

  1. 9:00
    Luggage forwardedSuitcases handed to the hotel desk. They arrive at the Kyoto hotel the next day, so the day is carried with a small bag only.
  2. 10:00
    Fujiko F. Fujio MuseumNoborito, 30 min from Shinjuku. Timed entry. Original drawings, the rooftop with Doraemon and the Anywhere Door, the museum cafe.
  3. 12:00
    Museum cafe lunchSee the picks below.
  4. 13:30
    Train to Shinagawa40 minutes.
  5. 14:30
    Shinkansen Nozomi to Kyoto2 hours 15. Mount Fuji on the right side about 45 minutes in, if the sky is clear.
  6. 17:00
    Check inKyoto hotel.
  7. 18:00
    Nishiki Market and GionThe 400-year-old food arcade, then the Hanamikoji lanes at dusk. Done by 20:00.

The route

Shinjuku → Noborito 30 min. Noborito → Shinagawa 40 min. Shinkansen 2h15. Kyoto Station → hotel by taxi. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Where we eat

Museum Cafe at Fujiko F. Fujio MuseumKawasaki · museum cafeDishes shaped like the characters, Anki-pan French toast, Dorayaki. Kids and adults both order dessert.Open in Google Maps ↗Nishiki MarketKyoto · street foodKyoto’s kitchen: grilled skewers, tamagoyaki, pickles, matcha sweets. Everyone picks their own along 400 metres.Open in Google Maps ↗

Good for Families. Anyone who wants the train ride to be part of the trip, not a transfer.

Keep in mind The Doraemon museum is closed Tuesdays and needs timed tickets. Shinkansen seats are reserved in advance for the group.

Day 5 · Kyoto · 50,000 volumes, then matcha

Kyoto Manga Museum and Uji

A former elementary school holding 50,000 manga volumes, read anywhere including the lawn. Then Uji, thirty minutes south: the home town of Kyoto Animation, the Byodo-in temple, and the best matcha in Japan.

The day

  1. 10:00
    Kyoto International Manga MuseumThe Wall of Manga, 200 metres of shelves in a 1929 school building. Pick a series, sit on the lawn, read. Portrait artists on weekends.
  2. 12:00
    LunchSee the picks below.
  3. 13:30
    Train to Uji30 minutes on the JR Nara line.
  4. 14:15
    Byodo-inThe Phoenix Hall on the 10-yen coin, 1053. The museum next door holds the original phoenix statues.
  5. 15:30
    Uji tea street and Nakamura TokichiMatcha parfait, matcha soba, the tea shops along the river.
  6. 16:30
    Kyoto Animation locationsThe Uji river bridges and the streets from Sound! Euphonium, for the fans in the group. Optional.
  7. 17:30
    Back to KyotoEvening free.

The route

Museum is central, walkable from most hotels. Kyoto → Uji 30 min by JR. Uji on foot. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Where we eat

Honke OwariyaKyoto · soba since 1465The oldest soba house in Kyoto, ten minutes from the museum. Hourai soba with eight toppings in a quiet old townhouse.Open in Google Maps ↗Nakamura Tokichi HontenUji · matchaThe 1854 tea merchant. Matcha jelly, matcha parfait, and tea to bring home. The garden seats go first.Open in Google Maps ↗

Good for Readers. Kyoto Animation fans. Guests who want a calm day between Tokyo and Osaka.

Keep in mind The Manga Museum closes Wednesdays. Byodo-in’s Phoenix Hall interior is a timed tour with limited places.

Kyoto International Manga Museum ↗ Byodo-in ↗

Day 6 · Kyoto → Osaka · Takarazuka to Den Den Town

Tezuka’s hometown, then Osaka

The Tezuka Osamu Manga Museum in Takarazuka, where the author of Astro Boy grew up, then Osaka’s own otaku district, Den Den Town, and Dotonbori street food at night.

The day

  1. 9:30
    Luggage forwardedTo the Osaka hotel, arriving the same evening.
  2. 10:30
    Train to TakarazukaOne hour from Kyoto.
  3. 11:30
    Tezuka Osamu Manga MuseumThe Phoenix at the entrance, Astro Boy, the animation workshop where you can make a short piece yourself.
  4. 13:30
    Train to Osaka Namba50 minutes.
  5. 14:30
    Den Den Town and Ota RoadOsaka’s Akihabara: figure shops, retro games, the maid cafes of Nipponbashi.
  6. 17:30
    DotonboriKushikatsu, okonomiyaki, the Glico sign, the canal lights.
  7. 19:00
    DoneHotel is minutes away.

The route

Kyoto → Takarazuka 1h by JR. Takarazuka → Namba 50 min. Den Den Town to Dotonbori is a 15-minute walk. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Where we eat

Kushikatsu Daruma DotonboriDotonbori · kushikatsu since 1929Deep-fried skewers, one dip only in the shared sauce. The angry-chef sign is the landmark.Open in Google Maps ↗MizunoDotonbori · okonomiyaki since 1945The yamaimo okonomiyaki cooked on the counter in front of you. Small, and worth the wait.Open in Google Maps ↗

Good for Guests who want both the origin story and the loud, delicious side of Osaka.

Keep in mind The Tezuka museum is closed Wednesdays. Dotonbori lines are shortest before 18:00.

Day 7 · Osaka · Two ways to finish

USJ, or a shopping finale

Option A is Universal Studios Japan: Super Nintendo World and the seasonal anime collaborations. Option B is a last day for collectors in Nipponbashi, with overseas shipping arranged from the post office. Then Kansai Airport or the train back to Tokyo.

The day

  1. 9:00
    Option A: Universal Studios JapanEarly entry for Super Nintendo World. Seasonal collaborations (Jujutsu Kaisen, Detective Conan, Attack on Titan) change every few months.
  2. 10:00
    Option B: collectors’ dayMandarake Grand Chaos, the Nipponbashi shops, then boxing and shipping purchases home from the post office.
  3. 15:00
    551 HoraiPork buns at Namba station, the Osaka thing to eat before a train.
  4. 16:00
    DepartureKansai Airport in 50 minutes by rapid train, or the Shinkansen back to Tokyo in 2 hours 30.

The route

Hotel → USJ 20 min by train. Namba → Kansai Airport 50 min. Namba → Shin-Osaka 15 min for the Shinkansen. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Where we eat

551 Horai NambaNamba · pork bunsSteamed pork buns made in front of the counter since 1945. Everyone in Osaka carries the white box.Open in Google Maps ↗Honke OhtakoDotonbori · takoyakiBig octopus pieces, crisp outside, soft inside, cooked at the stand. Eight for the price of a coffee.Open in Google Maps ↗

Good for Families choose A. Collectors choose B. Both end at the airport on time.

Keep in mind USJ Express passes for Nintendo World sell out early. Shipping from Japan Post takes 1 to 2 weeks by air.

How a departure gets fixed Two things set the calendar; everything else follows.

Ghibli Museum tickets go on sale on the 10th of the previous month at 10:00 Japan time and sell out within minutes. Once the target month is known, the trip dates are set around that day and I take care of the purchase.

Museum closing days differ by city (Tokiwa-so Mondays, Ghibli Tuesdays, Kyoto Manga Museum and the Tezuka museum Wednesdays). The order of days above already respects them; exact dates are checked again before confirming.

Shinkansen seats, timed museum entries and luggage forwarding are booked for the whole group in advance. Guests carry a day bag only.
